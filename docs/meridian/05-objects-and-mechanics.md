## Meridian Engine (A03)

- Story: built 1898 to test new Keepers; works only when two build it together. Rowan dismantled it.
- Two towers (Blue and Orange), each built from its own blueprint card as batches arrive. On Day 20 they build a shared bridge together.
- Both marble routes meet at one final cup ("Two must travel"). The first full run triggers a Professor call.
- Each tower route ends in numbered cups giving that child a final digit. **Backup:** the same digits sit under scratch-off on each blueprint card, unlocked if "the Engine is stubborn". Never make a vital clue depend only on marble routing.
- Build and test the full run before December.

## Atlas of Notes (A08)

- 1976 to 1986 the young Keepers took one note per country. Rowan kept her half; the Professor's half appears in his study photos.
- Each stop gives each child a **different** country's note on the same day, in a removable journal sleeve. About eight countries each.
- Choose 8 to 10 stops from the actual catalogue, forming a loop on the Usborne map that ends back in the UK. Nepal and India make a natural Asian leg.
- The last note (Day 21) is British, e.g. a real £5.
- On Christmas Eve the Atlas passes to the children to keep adding to.

## Recurring mechanics

- **Rule of Two:** everything comes in pairs or complementary halves (lenses, mirrors, photo halves, towers, jigsaws, padlocks, trails). No child ever holds a full answer alone.
- **Outbox:** reply slip in the basket overnight; next letter responds.
- **Red-filter notes:** cyan text under red noise, from Rowan.
- **Padlock box:** 14 to 16 days, one code per child.
- **Wool trails:** blue and orange, equal length and stop types.
- **Turn structures** for single shared items (egg, hat, LEGO): alternate instruction pages, or one holds while the other steers, then swap.

## Christmas Eve finale (outline)

1. Final briefing call.
2. Simultaneous blue and orange wool trails from the basket, collecting each child's last red-lens message, mirror message and digit.
3. Trails meet; combined messages give room plus spot.
4. Guardian placed on the jigsaw map; Court Figure symbols confirm; long key silhouette marks the spot.
5. Container hidden since 30 November under Nick's bed (blanket-wrapped box), locked with both padlocks. Stone glows on an LED puck; 1976 photo beneath. Both children lift it together.
6. Final call: Professor and Rowan together; spectacles on his head; children named Keepers 2026 to 2076; motto translated.
7. Certificates; parent takes the 2026 photograph mirroring 1976.
