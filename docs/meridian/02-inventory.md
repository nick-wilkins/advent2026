## D. Detective kit contents (STEM "Spy & Detective Experiments for Kids", confirmed from product photo)

Black hat, white gloves, detective ID holder with badge card, magnifying glass, 6-square fingerprint record pad, blue ink pad, fingerprint brush, powder puffer (white bulb with blue nozzle), cornstarch, iodophor dropper bottle, cotton swabs, clear tape, lift tape strip, pipette, graduated sample tube, small metal dish, measuring tape, tweezers, evidence bags, six yellow evidence markers (1 to 6), caution tape, rear-view spy glasses, invisible-ink pen with built-in UV light, tealight, booklet (20+ activities).

Useful techniques this kit supports:
- **UV invisible ink:** Rowan's secret second layer on ordinary-looking notes. The pen draws and reveals.
- **Starch and iodine writing:** write in cornstarch paste; dab diluted iodophor and the writing turns dark blue-black. Good for one supervised science day. Iodophor stains.
- **Fingerprints:** ink-pad prints on the record pad; dusting and lifting with powder, brush, puffer and tape.
- **Rear-view glasses:** "who is behind you" games and spotting a planted clue.
- **Tealight:** don't use it. If the booklet's heat-reveal ink is wanted, an adult uses a hairdryer instead.

Shared Order equipment. Children take turns with identical techniques; never one child gets the impressive items. Chemicals, glass, candles and tools need supervision; prefer alternatives to naked flames.
