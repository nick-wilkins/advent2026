# Characters

Part of the adopted story in `03-story.md`. Keep the speaking cast to two adults.

## Professor Thistlewick

- About fifty-six. Eccentric British explorer. Grey hair or beard, round spectacles, tweed waistcoat, warm intelligent face, old expedition gear.
- Warm, enthusiastic, clever but fallible, occasionally forgetful, proud. Genuinely relies on the children. Funny without being silly.
- Was **Agent Red** when made a Keeper in 1976, aged six (mirrors Flynn). His colour gives the red lenses their name.
- **Arc:** starts sure he can solve it himself and slightly hurt the clues are addressed to the children; ends grateful, humbled, and brave enough to call Rowan.
- He is a participant on the same trail, not a narrator of instructions. He depends on the children's answers (the Outbox).
- **Running jokes:** spectacles (always on his head; pays off in the final call); biscuit rankings from every country; Humphrey the camel (offstage, always to blame); calls cut off; remembering something important just as he hangs up; mistaking buttons for the kettle.
- **Serious register:** used sparingly, only when talking about Rowan.
- **Denial joke (Chapter II):** "R.A.? Could be anyone. Reginald Applegate. Ruth Abernathy."
- **Voice direction (ElevenLabs):** original warm, slightly breathless older British male voice, RP with a little crumble, quick when excited, softer in serious moments. Calls 20 to 90 seconds.

## Rowan Ashwood

- About fifty-eight. Explorer and the Professor's oldest friend and fellow Keeper. Was **Agent Green** in 1976, aged eight (mirrors Dylan).
- Quiet, precise, wry, never forgets anything.
- Apparent thief, actually the protector and the designer of the whole trail. She chose the children and slipped their names into the Order's records.
- **Delivery:** short handwritten-style notes, often hidden in red visual noise, signed "R.A.", always addressed to both children, always helpful. First audio around Day 18. Final call shared with the Professor.
- **Safety rules:** she never enters the house in the story, never threatens, is never described as watching the children.
- **Voice direction:** a calm, dry, older British female voice; a contrast to the Professor's bustle.

## Offstage and minor

- **Humphrey the camel:** anecdote only, never seen.
- **The Guardian:** the dragon on the Order emblem from Day 1; built from LEGO A04 in Chapter IV.
- **The Quartermaster (decided):** Nick, revealed on Christmas Eve as having hidden Rowan's sealed parcel.
- **Magnus Cogwell:** cut as a character. At most a name on the Engine blueprints ("Cogwell's Meridian Engine, 1898").

## Tone guardrails

- "Gone", never "broken into". No intruders, forced windows or anyone watching the house.
- The stakes are emotional (the Order ends without new Keepers), never physical danger.
- The Professor is never in peril; at worst he is stuck on a slow train with a rude goat.
- Each revelation resolves the same day it is set up, or the next.
